PNL PDF Reader: a free, private PDF reader in your browser

Open any PDF file and read it right in your web browser. There's nothing to install and no account to create. Your file is never uploaded: everything happens on your own device.

Features

  • Open a PDF by dragging it onto the page or choosing it from your computer.
  • Remembers your 5 most recent PDFs and the page you reached, so a refresh doesn't lose your place.
  • Search the text, zoom and rotate pages.
  • Fullscreen reading: press F to enter or leave fullscreen.
  • Click any word to look it up with the Dictionariez browser extension.

Are my PDF files uploaded anywhere?

No. The PDF is read and displayed entirely in your browser. No file data is sent over the internet.

How does it remember my recent files?

Your 5 most recent PDFs are saved in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB) on your device. They stay in that browser only, and you can remove one file or clear them all at any time.

Is it free?

Yes. The PDF Reader is free to use and doesn't require an account.

Can I open PDFs from websites like arXiv or Springer?

Yes, with the PNL Reader browser extension, which opens online PDFs in one click without downloading them first.
